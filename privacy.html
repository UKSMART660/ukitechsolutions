Privacy notice

This draft explains how UKITECHSOLUTIONS handles contact-form enquiries. The site owner should review it for their actual hosting, legal obligations, and retention practices before launch.

Information collected

The contact form asks for your name, email address, topic, and message. The server also stores a one-way hash derived from the connecting IP address to help limit abuse; the raw IP is not saved in the contact-message table.

Why it is used

Contact details and messages are used to respond to enquiries, provide requested assistance, and protect the form from abuse. If email notifications are configured, the message may also be sent to the business contact email.

Storage and retention

Enquiries are stored in the website's PostgreSQL database until an administrator deletes them or the site owner applies a retention policy. The hosting and database providers may process data to operate the service.

Security and sharing

The website is designed to use HTTPS, access-controlled admin accounts, and protected database sessions when deployed correctly. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Enquiry details should only be accessed by authorised personnel and are not intended for public display.
